# Campaign design references

Public references checked for the campaign library. These sources describe customer experiences and, where documented, editing tools; they do not reveal every competitor's internal admin system.

| Reference | Verified pattern | FastKirana application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Blinkit Brand Stores](https://blinkit.com/blog/introducing-brand-stores/) | Brands can create custom sections with their own design assets, preview changes in a simulator, and publish them. | Editable campaign content, live mobile preview, and separate draft/publication actions. |
| [Blinkit Ads catalogue](https://brands.blinkit.com/catalogue/) | The public catalogue lists banners, product shelves, brand spotlights, and brand stores as different advertising formats. | Different banner, product spotlight, category grid, and offer shelf compositions. |
| [Instamart's official app listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.swiggy.android.instamart) | Public app presentation shows grocery and other shopping collections. | Keep actual catalogue products and familiar category/search actions behind each campaign. |
| [Zepto's official app listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zeptoconsumerapp) | Public presentation separates daily groceries, broader shopping categories, café collections, gifting, and deals. | Provide everyday, festival, value, and seasonal starting themes rather than a single festive collection. |

The five FastKirana layouts are our implementation choices derived from these patterns. No private Swiggy or Zepto admin panel was inspected or claimed to be fully editable. Direct storefront inspection was limited by JavaScript/bot checks and the unavailable local browser runtime; official public listings and documentation were used instead. Competitor artwork, prices, delivery claims, and offer claims are not copied into these templates.
